Lake Opeka

An album design for Chicago musician Sean Fermoyle — cover, back, inner sleeve, centre labels, and a twelve-page booklet for his record, Lake Opeka. Every image was made with the Lab’s own tools: eight lake surfaces, one accent sunk under each, grown into fields of fused circles; the track titles set in dots.
