Lake Opeka

Album artwork for Chicago musician Sean Fermoyle’s record Lake Opeka — jacket, inner sleeve, center labels and a twelve-page booklet.

Rather than illustrate the record, we built the instruments that generate its artwork: custom tools that grow each page from a lake surface into a field of fused circles, and set every title as a screen of dots.
