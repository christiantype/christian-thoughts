Artifact  /  AI Experiment

BR AI NS

You are entering a slick showroom. Here, you will choose from lush cameo portraits—each with QR access to animated previews of highly desirable, even enviable, relationships. Be prepared for a most unique journey, but be warned: your selection says everything about you and your desires. Note, there may be things you didn’t know about yourself and certainly never intended to reveal to others.

Once you have chosen your bot, you can never unchoose it — for you are bot and sold. Playbot Companions / Generation 01 / Our branded techno-future.

OP/AL, in collaboration as BR AI NS, examined virtual companionship, desire, and AI identity through the lens of synthetic characters. The work was featured in two exhibitions in Toronto.

BR AI NS is an experimental collective interrogating artificial intelligence and its effects on culture, identity, and the human psyche. Their practice, anchored by the tagline "Intelligence, Interrupted," uses design as a form of critical inquiry into the techno-futures we are already living inside.

As of 2026, this experimental lab activity is in the design archive of the Art Institute of Chicago.
