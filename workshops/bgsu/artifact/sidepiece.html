Artifact  /  Typeface Design

Sidepiece

A bespoke typeface designed by Barry Deck, commissioned for Faculty, the Chicago Graphic Design Club's annual journal. Deck designed a seven-weight font family that considers typography’s place in today’s high-tech / high-touch world. Sidepiece has a point of view and is anchored in our shared culture. Below are Sidepiece’s inaugural outings — the spreads it sets in Faculty 3, the forthcoming issue.
