Artifact  /  Event Series

Confronting Design

An ongoing series co-produced with One Design that names what the field too often avoids — AI, values, danger — and invites practitioners to sit with the discomfort, question the conventions, and leave with something sharper.

Featuring voices across disciplines — Heather Snyder Quinn, Ashley Lukasik, Sharlene King, Steve Ryan, and John Pobojewski (design & technology); Nicolette Stosur-Bassett (brand strategy); Larry Tchogninou (design & architecture); Nermin Moufti (design director, Field of Practice); Tracy West (creative direction, 50,000feet); Annika Welander (studio ownership, Someoddpilot); Eric Hotchkiss (design-build, education); Nekita Thomas (design research, race & urban futures); Jordan Campbell (creative direction, Alt Space Chicago).
