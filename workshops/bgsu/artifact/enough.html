Enough

Los Angeles actor, Sonny Valicenti, delivers a screentest speaking one word thirty times. OP/AL created a custom tool to animate a series of subtitled words that float above the actor’s head carrying the campaign’s message through pace, rhythm, and emphasis.

ENOUGH2026 is a grassroots campaign uniting people against manufactured division and injustice under the rallying call "Together, we are Enough."
