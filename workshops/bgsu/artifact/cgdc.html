Chicago Graphic Design Club

Creating spaces for belonging and learning — opportunities for a community to gather, and to shape the industry and the identity of Chicago through design and conversation.

Through partnerships with thought leaders across the city and beyond, the CGDC has positioned itself as a leading voice in the industry — hosting events, publishing work, and creating space for honest dialogue about what design is, what it does, and where it's going.
