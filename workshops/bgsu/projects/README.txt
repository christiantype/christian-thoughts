Projects for slide 6 of the BGSU talk.

Drop images or films in this folder named by the order you want them shown:
  1.jpg, 2.png, 3.mp4, 4.webp ... (jpg, jpeg, png, webp, gif, mp4, mov)

The slide finds them by itself and plays one every 3 seconds, centred and uncropped.
No numbers missing in between: it stops looking at the first gap of three.
Until there is something here, the slide shows empty frames.
