Impression

Letterpress, struck by the voice. The type is fixed, as metal type is, and the voice is the press. How loud you say a word sets how deep it bites into the sheet; a voiced word carries ink and a whispered one goes down blind; holding a sound is dwell, and the ink squashes past the edge of the letter; a voice that slides in pitch slurs the impression, and a shout bruises the paper. Talk, or set the words first and strike them one at a time.
