VoiceType

Type set by the voice, as it is spoken. Each word is drawn from a variable font while you say it: loudness sets the weight and the ink, pitch moves the baseline so the contour of the sentence draws a line through it, how long you hold a sound stretches the width, and the pause before a word becomes the space in front of it. The prosody — the part of language that type has never carried — is the typography.
