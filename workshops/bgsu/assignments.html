Heart, Intuition and TechnologyDay one · Technology

The prompt

Your tool, first iteration

I want to create a tool that allows me to draw shapes by hand or generate them at random, color them, and scatter a poem across them, and it needs to include the following features:

  • Shapes I can draw and control myself, or have served to me at random.
  • Color for every shape: chosen by hand, one at a time, or picked for me from a palette.
  • A choice of canvas sizes, with a background color I can change.
  • Select any single shape and place an image inside it.
  • A field where I type a poem. The tool splits it into lines, words or letters and scatters them across the canvas.
  • A control for the scatter frequency: how much of the poem lands, and how far it spreads.
  • A choice of fonts for the poem.

And I want to be able to export it to be used for a tabloid poster.

Heart, Intuition and TechnologyDay two · Technology

Day two

Build a tool that includes image, sound, type, color, shape, motion

Start from your constraints and your prompt. Then print five posters, 11 × 17, in color. If you need a way in, imagine if…

  • every O vibrated like my breath.
  • every period was a different color.
  • the spaces between words could sing.
  • every vowel melted in the sun.
  • a poster changed with the weather outside.
  • your voice became a typeface as you spoke.
  • commas were seeds.
