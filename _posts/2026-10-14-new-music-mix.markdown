A new mix today for [Mu Radio](https://www.mixcloud.com/ChicagoGraphicDesignClub/) by the [Chicago Graphic Design Club](https://chicagographicdesign.club/).

<iframe class="audio-embed" width="100%" height="120" src="https://www.mixcloud.com/widget/iframe/?hide_cover=1&light=1&feed=%2FChicagoGraphicDesignClub%2F10-14-2026-set-by-christian-solorzano%2F" frameborder="0" loading="lazy" allow="autoplay" title="Mu Radio — 10-14-2026 • Set by Christian Solorzano"></iframe>

Featuring Chloé Antoniotti, Kelly Lee Owens, Okonski, Max Cooper, Peach, Vince Tempera &amp; Franco Bixio, Gardens of God, Eraldo Bernocchi &amp; Harold Budd, Jansen &amp; Barbieri, Shida Shahabi, Omni Gardens, Sofie Birch and Simon Jeffes.

Thirteen tracks, fifty-six minutes, mixed so it runs without a break.

I also made an animation for it. Every shape in it is drawn by the music — one sample from each track, each with its own colour and its own figure.
