--- layout: default --- {%- comment -%} Sound — the sketches, newest first. The entries come from _data/sketches.yml and nothing else. A file dropped in assets/audio/ does NOT appear here on its own, which is deliberate: the photography page builds itself from whatever is on disk and that is right for photographs, but a sound with no date, no place and no sentence beside it is not a sketch yet — it is a file. Writing the entry is the publishing step. The list is set like the lists on /about/: the quiet line first, the name under it. Same markup, same classes, so there is one list on this site and not two. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} The ground. It is empty until something plays — an analyser has nothing to read otherwise — so the page at rest is still just the list. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} The stage. Everything but the ground goes away and the sound plays straight through, sketch after sketch, each one bringing its own background. Three controls and nothing else; they fade out when the pointer stops and come back when it moves. {%- endcomment -%}
{%- comment -%} No heading over the list. The page is called Sound in the menu and the entries name themselves; a word sitting above two rows is labelling something already in plain sight. {%- endcomment -%}
{%- comment -%} The list sits on a plate. Over Blocks or Scan the ground goes to solid colour behind the type, and without something between them the dates stop being readable — which is the one real risk of a full-bleed visualiser. The plate is nearly invisible until it is needed. {%- endcomment -%}
